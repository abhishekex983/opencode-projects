What Does It Mean When
Your Hair Is Thinning?
Here's What's Really Going On
What Is It?
Thinning hair means individual strands are becoming finer or less dense, reducing your hair's overall volume. It's not the same as balding — it's a gradual change in thickness and coverage.
Common Causes
  • Genetics — up to 80% of men, 50% of women
  • Hormonal shifts — pregnancy, menopause, thyroid
  • Significant stress — physical or emotional
  • Nutritional deficiencies — iron, vitamin D, protein
  • Medical conditions — alopecia, autoimmune disorders
Signs to Watch For
  • A wider hair part than before
  • More visible scalp through your hair
  • A ponytail that feels less full
Can It Grow Back?
  • Minoxidil — FDA-approved topical treatment
  • Scalp health — clean, inflammation-free environment
  • Regrowth is often possible depending on the cause
