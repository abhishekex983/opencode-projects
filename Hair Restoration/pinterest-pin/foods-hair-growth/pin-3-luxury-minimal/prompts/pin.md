## Design Principles
- Premium editorial magazine cover feel
- Strong visual hierarchy: headline → image → subtitle → brand
- Designed for Pinterest scrolling — must stop the scroll
- Generous whitespace, elegant spacing
- All text perfectly spelled and grammatically correct
- Maximum 3 text elements: headline, subtitle, brand name

## What to AVOID
- Generic stock infographic layouts
- Excessive icons or bullet points
- Cartoon illustrations
- Cluttered information blocks
- Fake statistics on the image
- Small unreadable text
