Niche Research

Before committing to a niche, see who’s already winning with low domain authority. Enter a niche → we expand it into keywords → harvest the top SERPs → surface the underdog domains (low DA, real traffic) ranking inside it → Firecrawl each one to tag how they monetize (AdSense, Mediavine, Ezoic, Amazon, affiliate networks, ecommerce). Sort by Perf — traffic-per-DA — to find the David-beating-Goliath sites worth studying or outranking.
