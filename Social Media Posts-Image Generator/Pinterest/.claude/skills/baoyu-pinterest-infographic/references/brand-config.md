# Brand Configuration: Thin Hair Growth Guide

## Brand Identity

**Brand Name**: Thin Hair Growth Guide
**Website**: thinhairgrowthguide.com
**Target Audience**: Adults experiencing hair thinning/loss
**Tone**: Medical-trust, clean, authoritative

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary (dark navy) | `#002444` | Main titles, cell headers, large text |
| Secondary (red) | `#b91c1c` | Accent highlights, key statistics, warning icons |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | White/cream background |
| Line art | `#000000` | All icons and illustrations |
| Body text | `#374151` | Supporting text |

## Typography

- **Font Family**: Sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial, or similar)
- **Title**: Bold, prominent, dark navy (#002444)
- **Cell Headers**: Bold, dark navy (#002444)
- **Body Text**: Regular weight, dark gray (#374151)
- **Statistics**: Large, bold, dark navy with red accent

## Visual Style

- **Icons**: Minimal line art (IKEA manual style)
- **No photographs**: Line icons only
- **Layout**: Bento grid with clear cell boundaries
- **Whitespace**: Ample, for visual clarity
- **Hierarchy**: Through cell size variation

## Content Guidelines

### Tone
- Medical-trust and authoritative
- Clean and modern
- Action-oriented (especially for male audience)
- Scannable and easy to digest

### Statistics
- Always use verbatim data from source
- Highlight with red (#b91c1c) accent
- Large, bold numbers for impact

### Call-to-Action
- Direct and action-oriented
- Examples: "Act early.", "Early detection = better outcomes"
- Include website URL in footer

## Pinterest Optimization

- **Aspect Ratio**: 2:3 (1080x1620px)
- **Orientation**: Portrait
- **Mobile-first**: Large text, clear icons
- **Scannable**: Quick visual hierarchy
- **Engagement**: Curiosity-driven headlines
