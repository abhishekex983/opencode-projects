# How to Improve the Health of Thinning Men's Hair

**Author**: Abhishek Bolar, Certified Hair Care Writer
**Published**: August 20, 2026

## TL;DR

Improving the health of thinning men's hair requires a combination of proper nutrition, a consistent hair care routine, and the right treatments. Eating nutrient-dense foods like pumpkin seeds, sardines, spinach, and eggs supplies the vitamins and minerals your hair follicles need to grow stronger. Using thickening shampoos like Nioxin and topical treatments such as minoxidil and finasteride can help slow thinning and support regrowth. Scalp massage and blood flow stimulation add another layer of support. Start early, stay consistent, and talk to a dermatologist about what makes sense for your situation.

## Key Takeaways

1. Eat for your hair — protein, zinc, iron, omega-3 fatty acids
2. Use products that support your routine — Nioxin, Nizoral
3. Consider topical treatments with doctor's guidance
4. Stimulate your scalp — massage and derma rolling
