## Summary

Eating the right foods provides your hair with the protein, omega-3 fatty acids, iron, zinc, and antioxidants it needs to grow strong and healthy. Nutritional deficiencies — especially in vitamins B12, D, biotin, iron, and zinc — can lead to hair loss and slow growth.

## Key Foods

### Protein-Rich Foods
- Eggs: rich in biotin, helps produce keratin
- Sardines: protein + omega-3 fatty acids + iron
- Hair is mostly made of keratin (protein)

### Seeds and Nuts
- Pumpkin seeds: rich in zinc (supports hair growth/repair cycle)
- Walnuts: omega-3 fatty acids + vitamin E
- Flaxseeds: 4.7g omega-3 per 2 tablespoons

### Leafy Greens and Berries
- Spinach: vitamins A and C, iron, folate
- Blueberries: vitamin C (antioxidant, protects follicles)
- Strawberries: 113% daily vitamin C per cup

## Daily Meal Plan
- **Breakfast**: Oatmeal + blueberries + walnuts
- **Lunch**: Sardines + rice + eggs
- **Afternoon**: Eggs + rice + spinach
- **Snack**: Pumpkin seeds + barley tea/coffee/chai

## Key Takeaways
1. Protein is foundational — hair is mostly keratin
2. Seeds and nuts deliver zinc and omega-3s
3. Leafy greens and berries provide vitamins and antioxidants
4. Consistency matters more than quantity
5. Diet is one piece of the puzzle — consult a healthcare professional
