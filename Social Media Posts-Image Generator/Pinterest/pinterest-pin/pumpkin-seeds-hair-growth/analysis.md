# Content Analysis

## Topic
Pumpkin seeds for hair growth — personal experience with dietary approach to hair health.

## Tone
Informative, personal, first-person narrative. Author shares own experience without medical claims.

## Key Points
- Personal switch from topical pumpkin seed oil to eating seeds
- Nutrients: zinc (7.5mg/100g), beta-sitosterol (265mg/100g), omega-6, vitamin E
- Clinical study: 40% hair count increase over 24 weeks (400mg daily pumpkin seed oil)
- Practical tips: snack, oatmeal addition, raw > roasted
- Consistency over quantity

## Target Audience
Men experiencing hair thinning or early hair loss. Site is "Thin Hair Growth Guide" — male-focused hair health content.

## Niche
Men's hair loss / nutrition / natural remedies
