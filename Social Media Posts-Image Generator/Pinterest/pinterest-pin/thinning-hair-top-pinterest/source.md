# What to Do for Thinning Hair on Top of Head: My Experience

**Source:** Blog post (content provided by user)
**Author:** Abhishek Bolar
**Topic:** Thinning hair on top of head, crown thinning

## TL;DR

To address thinning hair on top of your head, research suggests starting with minoxidil — the most-studied topical treatment for crown thinning. A systematic review in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology found minoxidil and finasteride both effective for promoting hair growth, with minoxidil available over the counter. If you have a double crown like me, it can be hard to tell whether the area looks sparse due to normal patterning or actual thinning. A dermatologist can confirm the cause and recommend the right approach for your situation.

## Key Points

### Why Hair Thins on Top First
- Hair thinning on the crown often follows a pattern tied to genetics
- The vertex, or crown, tends to show changes earlier for many people
- Family history plays a clear role
- The top area becomes more visible under certain lighting
- Double crown can make it harder to separate normal variation from real thinning

### What I Did: From Essential Oils to Medical Treatment
- Started with castor oil and olive oil (few times a week)
- Added derma rolling on some weekends
- Results stayed modest
- Spoke with a dermatologist who prescribed Xyon gel
- Tracked photos every four weeks
- Goal was thickening rather than expecting dramatic regrowth

### Making Thinning Hair on Top Look Thicker
- Shorter sides with more length on top created better balance
- Layered cuts around the crown area gave illusion of more volume
- Texture sprays and light powders added grip
- Very short styles sometimes highlighted the thinning more than medium lengths
- Goal remained looking natural rather than covering anything up

### When to See a Dermatologist
- Double crown made it tough to judge whether thinning or simply patterned
- Professional exam removed uncertainty
- Visit included photos and scalp check
- Left with a plan that fit schedule
- Discussing family history helped understand likely progression

## Key Takeaways
- **Crown thinning is common** — the top of the head is often where male pattern baldness shows first due to genetics and DHT sensitivity
- **Minoxidil is the first-line treatment** — available over the counter, research-backed for crown thinning
- **Essential oils and derma rolling may help in early stages** — research is limited, but they're low-risk starting points
- **A double crown can mimic thinning** — get a proper diagnosis before starting any treatment
- **See a dermatologist first** — a professional can confirm the cause and recommend the right approach
