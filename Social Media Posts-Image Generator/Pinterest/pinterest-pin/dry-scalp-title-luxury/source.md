# How to Slow Hair Thinning: What I've Learned Over the Years

**Source:** https://thinhairgrowthguide.com/how-to-slow-hair-thinning/
**Author:** Abhishek Bolar
**Date:** August 13, 2026
**Read Time:** 7 min read

## TL;DR

To slow hair thinning, a systematic review and meta-analysis published in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology found that minoxidil, finasteride, and low-level laser light therapy are all effective for promoting hair growth — all superior to placebo. Cleveland Clinic specialists identify topical minoxidil and oral finasteride as the first-line treatments for male pattern hair loss. Neither treatment works for everyone, and both require continuous use to maintain results. A dermatologist can diagnose the underlying cause and recommend the right approach for your specific situation.

## Key Points

### Understanding Why Hair Thinning Happens
- Hair thinning is incredibly common
- Several factors contribute: genetics, hormones, stress, diet, and age
- For the author, genetics was the biggest factor (male pattern baldness)
- Male pattern baldness runs in the family, started noticing changes in mid-twenties
- Hair thinning is often so gradual that you don't realize how much has changed until you compare over time
- If you're just starting to notice something, that's actually the best time to act

### Early Maintenance: Oils, Massages, and Better Shampoos
- Started using natural oils like castor oil and olive oil
- Castor oil: moisturizes the scalp and creates a healthier environment for hair growth
- Olive oil: general conditioning, helped with dryness, made hair feel thicker
- Scalp massages became part of weekly routine (10-15 minutes)
- Research suggests regular scalp massages may help with hair thickness
- Upgraded shampoo game: started investing in shampoos designed for thinning hair
- For a while, this routine worked well enough
- Cleveland Clinic notes that hair loss can be influenced by a wide range of factors, and early intervention often leads to better outcomes

### When Maintenance Isn't Enough: Moving to Medical Solutions
- By early thirties, natural oils and better shampoos weren't cutting it anymore
- Went to a dermatologist and had a proper consultation
- Discussed family history, pattern of thinning, and what was already tried
- Hair thinning can also be influenced by stress-related hair loss
- At 34 years old, the most effective approach was using Xyon gel
- Xyon gel: topical solution combining minoxidil and finasteride in a gel form
- Using it consistently has helped with thickening hair and regrowing some of what was lost
- When hope was lost, Xyon gel turned things around

### Diet, Stress, and Lifestyle Factors
- Diet and lifestyle affect hair health significantly
- Started incorporating more foods that support blood circulation and provide nutrients hair follicles need
- Foods: spinach, eggs, fatty fish, nuts
- Started taking a multivitamin to fill in gaps
- Benefits of finasteride for addressing the hormonal component of hair loss
- Slowing hair thinning isn't about one magic solution
- It's about layering multiple approaches: right products, right diet, stress management, professional guidance

## Key Takeaways
- **Start early** — the sooner you address hair thinning, the more options you have
- **Minoxidil and finasteride** are the most research-backed treatments, but both require continuous use
- **Maintenance matters** — oils, scalp massages, and quality shampoos can help in the early stages
- **Diet and stress play a role** — nutrient deficiencies and chronic stress can accelerate thinning
- **See a dermatologist** — a professional can diagnose the cause and recommend the right approach for your situation
