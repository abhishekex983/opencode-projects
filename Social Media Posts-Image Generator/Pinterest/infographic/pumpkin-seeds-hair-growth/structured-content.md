# Pumpkin Seeds for Hair Growth: Nutrients, Research & How to Eat

## Overview
An infographic breaking down the key nutrients in pumpkin seeds that support hair growth, the clinical research behind them, and practical ways to add them to your daily diet.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. Which nutrients in pumpkin seeds support hair growth
2. What clinical research says about pumpkin seed oil effectiveness
3. How to incorporate pumpkin seeds into daily meals

## Section 2: Zinc

**Key Concept**: Essential mineral for hair growth

**Content**:
- 7.5mg of zinc per 100 grams of pumpkin seeds
- Zinc plays a role in protein synthesis and cell division
- Both necessary for hair growth
- Zinc deficiency can contribute to hair loss

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "ZINC"
- Value: "7.5mg / 100g"
- Description: "Protein synthesis & cell division"

---

## Section 3: Beta-Sitosterol

**Key Concept**: Plant sterol that may block DHT

**Content**:
- 265mg of phytosterols per 100 grams
- May influence enzyme 5-alpha-reductase
- Involved in conversion of testosterone to DHT

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "BETA-SITOSTEROL"
- Value: "265mg / 100g"
- Description: "Blocks 5-alpha-reductase enzyme"

---

## Section 4: Omega-6 Fatty Acids

**Key Concept**: Anti-inflammatory for scalp health

**Content**:
- Linoleic acid supports overall scalp health
- May help reduce inflammation around hair follicles

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "OMEGA-6"
- Description: "Scalp health & anti-inflammatory"

---

## Section 5: Vitamin E

**Key Concept**: Antioxidant protection

**Content**:
- Acts as antioxidant protecting cells from oxidative stress
- Supplementation improved hair growth in studies

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "VITAMIN E"
- Description: "Antioxidant protection"

---

## Section 6: Clinical Research

**Key Concept**: Evidence-based results

**Content**:
- Participants took 400mg of pumpkin seed oil daily
- Duration: 24 weeks
- Result: 40% increase in hair count
- Placebo group: only 10% increase
- Self-rated improvement significantly higher in treatment group

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "CLINICAL STUDY"
- Main number: "40%"
- Context: "Hair count increase in 24 weeks"
- Comparison: "vs 10% placebo"

---

## Section 7: How to Eat

**Key Concept**: Practical daily habits

**Content**:
- As a snack: grab a handful anytime
- In oatmeal: mix with walnuts and blueberries
- Raw vs roasted: raw retains more nutrients
- Consistency matters more than quantity
- Daily handful > occasional large amounts

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "HOW TO EAT"
- Tips: "Snack", "In oatmeal", "Raw > roasted", "Daily handful"
