## TL;DR
Thinning hair happens because of genetics, hormonal changes, nutritional deficiencies, stress, or medical conditions like thyroid disease and alopecia areata. In my case, genetics played the biggest role. I tried changing my diet, taking supplements, and using natural oils before finding a treatment that actually worked for me.

## The Main Causes of Thinning Hair

### 1. Genetics (Androgenetic Alopecia)
Most common cause worldwide. Hair follicles shrink and eventually stop growing hair. Can start as early as teens.

### 2. Hormonal Changes and DHT
Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) derived from testosterone shrinks hair follicles over time. Main culprit behind male pattern baldness.

### 3. Nutritional Deficiencies
Iron, folic acid, zinc, biotin, protein, and vitamin D all support healthy hair growth. Deficiencies contribute to thinning.

### 4. Stress and Telogen Effluvium
Chronic stress pushes hair follicles into resting phase. Usually temporary — hair grows back within 6-9 months.

### 5. Medical Conditions and Medications
Thyroid disease, alopecia areata, scalp infections. Medications like chemotherapy, blood pressure drugs.

### 6. Lifestyle Factors
Tight hairstyles, chemical treatments, harsh products, heated styling tools.

## Key Statistics
- More than 50% of females will experience noticeable hair loss (Cleveland Clinic)
- Female pattern hair loss affects ~30 million in the US
- Catching hair loss early makes a big difference in treatment outcomes
- Hair cycles: anagen (growing) → catagen (transition) → telogen (resting)
