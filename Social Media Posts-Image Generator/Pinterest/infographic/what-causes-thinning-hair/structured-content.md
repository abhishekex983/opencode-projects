## Data Points (Verbatim)

### Statistics
- "approximately 85% of men will experience significant hair thinning by age 50"
- "Male pattern baldness affects about 50 million men in the United States"
- "25% of men begin losing hair before age 21"

### Key Terms
- **Androgenetic Alopecia**: Genetic hair loss condition (male pattern baldness)
- **DHT (Dihydrotestosterone)**: Hormone derived from testosterone that shrinks hair follicles
- **Male Pattern Baldness**: Receding hairline and thinning at the crown
