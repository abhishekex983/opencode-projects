## Main Topic

Explains the 6 main causes of hair thinning — genetics, hormonal changes (DHT), nutritional deficiencies, stress, medical conditions, and lifestyle factors — based on personal experience and research.

## Learning Objectives

After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. The most common causes of hair thinning
2. How genetics and hormones (DHT) contribute to hair loss
3. The role of nutrition, stress, and lifestyle in hair health

## Target Audience

- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner
- **Context**: People experiencing hair thinning who want to understand the causes
- **Expectations**: Quick, scannable health guidance with key statistics

## Content Type Analysis

- **Data Structure**: Cause list with supporting statistics
- **Key Relationships**: Multiple causes contribute to one condition (thinning hair)
- **Visual Opportunities**: Icon grid for causes, stat callout for the 50%+ figure

## Key Data Points (Verbatim)

- "more than 50% of females will experience noticeable hair loss at some point"
- "Female pattern hair loss alone affects about 30 million people in the United States"
- "Hair goes through three cycles: the anagen phase (growing), the catagen phase (transition), and the telogen phase (resting)"
- "telogen effluvium is usually temporary. Once the stress passes, hair often grows back within 6 to 9 months"
