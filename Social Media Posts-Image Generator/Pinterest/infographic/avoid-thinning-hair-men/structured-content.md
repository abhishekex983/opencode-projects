## Data Points (Verbatim)

### Statistics
- "approximately 50% of men are affected by some form of hair loss by age 50"
- "about 20% of men start balding by age 20"
- "about 30% by age 30"
- "The earlier it starts, the more aggressive it tends to be"

### Key Terms
- **Androgenetic Alopecia**: Genetic hair loss condition (male pattern baldness)
- **DHT (Dihydrotestosterone)**: Hormone derived from testosterone that shrinks hair follicles
- **Minoxidil**: Topical treatment that promotes hair regrowth
- **Finasteride**: DHT blocker available as topical gel

### Diet Nutrients
- **Pumpkin seeds**: Omega fatty acids, zinc, magnesium
- **Sardines**: Omega-3 fatty acids, protein, vitamin D, B12
- **Eggs**: Protein, biotin, selenium
- **Spinach**: Iron, folate, vitamins A and C
- **Walnuts**: Biotin, vitamin E, zinc

### Other Requirements
- Logo bottom-right corner
