## Main Topic

Explains how dry scalp contributes to hair thinning through inflammation and follicle damage, covering common causes, treatments, and when to see a dermatologist.

## Learning Objectives

After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. How dry scalp causes hair thinning (mechanism: dry → itch → scratch → follicle damage)
2. The 5 common causes of dry scalp
3. Available treatments and when to seek professional help

## Target Audience

- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner
- **Context**: People experiencing hair thinning who suspect scalp health is a factor
- **Expectations**: Quick, scannable health guidance with actionable takeaways

## Key Data Points (Verbatim)

- "as many as 32% of people with hair loss also report having a sensitive, irritated scalp"
- Treatments: zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole, salicylic acid
- Hydrating ingredients: glycerin, aloe vera, natural oils
- Scalp oils: tea tree oil, jojoba oil, coconut oil
