## Key Points

### How Dry Scalp Causes Hair Thinning

- Dry scalp happens when your scalp doesn't produce enough natural oils (sebum)
- Without enough moisture, the skin becomes tight, itchy, and flaky
- The main way dry scalp leads to hair loss is through inflammation
- When your scalp is dry and irritated, you scratch it
- Scratching damages the delicate hair follicles underneath
- Over time, this damage causes follicles to produce thinner, weaker hairs
- Research suggests that as many as 32% of people with hair loss also report having a sensitive, irritated scalp
- The constant scratching creates micro-tears in the scalp, which leads to inflammation
- This inflammation can push hair follicles into a resting phase, causing them to stop producing new hair
- When your scalp is dry, its protective barrier gets compromised
- This makes it easier for irritants and bacteria to get in

### Common Causes of Dry Scalp

- **Vitamin Deficiencies:** Low levels of vitamin D, iron, or biotin can affect scalp health
- **Harsh Hair Products:** Shampoos with sulfates, alcohol, or other harsh chemicals strip natural oils
- **Weather Changes:** Cold, dry winter air; indoor heating makes it worse
- **Stress:** More cortisol production disrupts oil production in your scalp
- **Over-Washing:** Washing hair too often strips away natural oils

### Treatments

- **Medicated Shampoos:** Zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole, or salicylic acid target yeast and inflammation
- **Moisturizing Products:** Glycerin, aloe vera, or natural oils help replenish moisture
- **Gentle Hair Care:** Avoid harsh chemicals, heat styling, and over-washing
- **Scalp Treatments:** Tea tree oil, jojoba oil, or coconut oil reduce inflammation
- **Lifestyle Changes:** Managing stress, balanced diet, staying hydrated

### When to See a Dermatologist

- Persistent itching that doesn't go away with regular shampoos
- Redness, swelling, or bleeding on your scalp
- Hair loss that's getting worse despite treatment
- Flaking that's severe or doesn't respond to dandruff shampoos

### Can Hair Grow Back?

- If damage is temporary and caught early, hair can often grow back
- Key is to treat the underlying cause before damage becomes permanent
- If damage has been going on for a long time, it might be harder to reverse

### Statistics

- "as many as 32% of people with hair loss also report having a sensitive, irritated scalp" (PMC study)

### Key Terms

- **Sebum**: Natural oils produced by the scalp
- **Malassezia**: Naturally-occurring yeast that can cause dandruff
- **Seborrheic Dermatitis**: Chronic inflammatory skin condition affecting the scalp
