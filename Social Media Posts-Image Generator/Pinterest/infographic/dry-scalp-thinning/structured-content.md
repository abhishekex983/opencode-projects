# Can Dry Scalp Cause Thinning Hair?

## Overview

A Pinterest-optimized infographic explaining the connection between dry scalp and hair thinning, covering the mechanism, common causes, treatments, and when to see a dermatologist.

## Learning Objectives

The viewer will understand:
1. How dry scalp causes hair thinning (the chain reaction)
2. The 5 common causes of dry scalp
3. Available treatments and warning signs to see a dermatologist

## Cell 2: The Mechanism (2x1)

**Key Concept**: Dry scalp triggers a chain reaction leading to hair thinning.

**Content**:
- Step 1: Dry scalp (insufficient sebum)
- Step 2: Itching and irritation
- Step 3: Scratching damages follicles
- Step 4: Follicles produce thinner, weaker hair

**Visual Element**:
- Type: 4-step chain with arrows
- Subject: 4 minimal line icons connected by arrows: dry skin → itch symbol → scratch hand → thin hair strand
- Treatment: Linear left-to-right flow, each step a small circle with icon

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "How It Happens"
- Step labels: "Dry Scalp" → "Itching" → "Scratching" → "Thinner Hair"

## Cell 3: Common Causes (2x2)

**Key Concept**: Five common factors that cause dry scalp.

**Content**:
- Vitamin Deficiencies: Low vitamin D, iron, or biotin
- Harsh Hair Products: Sulfates, alcohol strip natural oils
- Weather Changes: Cold air, indoor heating
- Stress: Cortisol disrupts oil production
- Over-Washing: Strips away natural oils

**Visual Element**:
- Type: 5 icon grid (2 top row, 3 bottom row or staggered)
- Subject: Pill/vitamin icon, bottle icon, snowflake icon, brain/stress icon, water/shower icon
- Treatment: Simple line icons, each in its own small cell

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "Common Causes"
- Labels: "Vitamin Deficiency", "Harsh Products", "Weather", "Stress", "Over-Washing"

## Cell 4: Treatments (2x1)

**Key Concept**: Four approaches to treat dry scalp.

**Content**:
- Medicated Shampoos: Zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole, salicylic acid
- Moisturizing Products: Glycerin, aloe vera, natural oils
- Gentle Hair Care: Avoid harsh chemicals, heat styling
- Scalp Treatments: Tea tree oil, jojoba oil, coconut oil

**Visual Element**:
- Type: 4 horizontal items with icons
- Subject: Shampoo bottle, drop/moisture icon, gentle hand icon, oil bottle icon
- Treatment: Row of 4 icons with labels underneath

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "Treatments That Help"
- Labels: "Medicated Shampoos", "Moisturize", "Gentle Care", "Scalp Oils"
