## Design Rules
- Thin black lines or subtle gaps between cells
- Each cell has own background (white or very light gray variation)
- Consistent padding inside each cell (16-24px)
- Cell headers: bold, dark navy (#002444), 18-24px
- Subheads: regular, dark gray (#374151), 14-16px
- Statistics: large, bold, 48-64px
- Icons: minimal line art only, black (#000000), 48-64px
- No photographs, no complex illustrations, no fills or shading on icons
- All text must be perfectly spelled and grammatically correct
- Mobile-first: large text, clear icons, scannable hierarchy
