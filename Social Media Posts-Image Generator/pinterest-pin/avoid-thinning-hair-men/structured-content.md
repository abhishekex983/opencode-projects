# Structured Pin Content

## Headline
**STOP THINNING HAIR NOW**

- Max 6 words
- Direct, bold, urgency-driven
- Targets men actively searching for solutions
- Male audience: no fluff, straight to the point

## Subtitle
**Real diet and routine that actually worked for me**

- Max 10 words
- Personal credibility signal
- Promises actionable, tested advice
- Differentiates from generic health content

## Brand Name
**THIN HAIR GROWTH GUIDE**

- Uppercase, wide letter-spacing
- Bottom placement
- Matches logo brand identity

## Visual Direction
- Style: editorial-clean (Men's Health aesthetic)
- Colors: Dark navy (#1B3A5C) background, red accent (#C0392B), white text
- Hero image: before/after hair care photo from blog
- Composition: headline top, hero middle, subtitle lower, brand bottom
