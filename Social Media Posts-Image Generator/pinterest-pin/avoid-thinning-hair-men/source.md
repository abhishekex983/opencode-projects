# How to Avoid Thinning Hair as a Man: My Diet, Routine, and What Actually Worked

Source: https://thinhairgrowthguide.com/how-to-avoid-thinning-hair-as-a-man/

## Summary

Men can slow hair thinning by eating a nutrient-rich diet with protein, omega-3s, zinc, and iron, maintaining a consistent hair care routine with thickening shampoos and scalp treatments, and addressing stress and vitamin deficiencies early. Genetics play a major role in male pattern baldness, so early intervention is critical — the sooner you act, the more hair you can preserve.

## Key Topics

1. **Causes of Hair Thinning in Men** — androgenetic alopecia (male pattern baldness), nutritional deficiencies, stress, poor scalp health, tight hairstyles, medical conditions
2. **Personal Journey** — olive oil phase (2018), onion juice experiment (2019), more serious treatments (2021), turning point with topical minoxidil/finasteride (2025)
3. **Diet for Hair Health** — pumpkin seeds, sardines, eggs, spinach, walnuts
4. **Hair Care Routine** — alternate day washing, Nioxin System Kit, Nizoral anti-dandruff, Xyon gel (minoxidil+finasteride), scalp massage
5. **What Didn't Work** — olive oil alone, onion juice, rosemary oil, derma rolling alone, supplements without blood work
6. **Supplements Worth Considering** — vitamin D, iron (with blood test), biotin, saw palmetto, omega-3s
7. **When to See a Dermatologist** — early intervention is key

## Key Takeaways

- Start early — easier to slow than reverse
- Eat for your hair — protein, omega-3s, iron, zinc, vitamin D
- Build a consistent routine
- Don't rely on natural remedies alone for male pattern baldness
- Get blood work done before supplements
