# Blog Content Analysis

## Topic
Men's hair thinning prevention and personal regrowth journey — diet, routine, and evidence-based treatments.

## Tone
Personal, honest, practical. Written from direct experience with male pattern baldness. Not preachy — shares both successes and failures.

## Key Points
- Male pattern baldness affects ~50% of men by age 50
- Diet (protein, omega-3s, zinc, iron, vitamin D) provides foundation
- Consistent routine with thickening products helps appearance
- Topical minoxidil + finasteride provided actual regrowth after 1 year
- Natural remedies (oils, derma rolling) alone were insufficient
- Blood work before supplements is essential
- Early intervention with a dermatologist is critical

## Target Audience
Men experiencing hair thinning or early-stage male pattern baldness, ages 20-45, looking for practical, no-BS advice.

## Niche
Men's hair health, grooming, wellness

## Recommended Pin Style
editorial-clean — matches the Men's Health / personal health blog aesthetic

## Recommended Audience
male — direct, bold tone with strong contrast visuals

## Headline Angle
Focus on the actionable "how to stop thinning" angle — men searching for this are in problem-solving mode.
