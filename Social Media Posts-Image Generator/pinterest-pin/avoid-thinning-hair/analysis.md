# Content Analysis

## Topic
Male hair thinning prevention — personal journey with diet, routine, and treatments

## Tone
Personal, authoritative, evidence-based. First-person narrative with medical references.

## Key Points
1. Male pattern baldness affects 50% of men by age 50 — early action critical
2. Diet (protein, omega-3s, zinc, iron) provides foundation for hair health
3. Consistent routine (thickening shampoo, scalp treatment) improves appearance
4. Natural remedies alone insufficient for genetic hair loss
5. Topical minoxidil + finasteride delivered actual regrowth after 1 year
6. Get blood work done before supplementing

## Audience
Men 25-50 experiencing early to moderate hair thinning. Direct, no-nonsense tone. Action-oriented.

## Niche
Men's health, hair restoration, grooming, wellness

## Pin Angle
The urgency of early intervention + proven routine that actually works. Personal credibility (tried everything, found what works).

## Visual Direction
Medical-authoritative style. Before/after hero image. Clean, clinical, trust-building. Dark navy + red accent.
